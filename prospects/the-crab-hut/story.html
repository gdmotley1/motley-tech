Our story

Back to His First Love

Qwan Butts traveled the world as a welder. Now he cooks for Milledgeville.

The road

Cooking Shows and Welding Contracts

Qwan grew up loving to cook and watching cooking shows, even though he thought back then that cooking wasn't a job for a man. He became a welder instead and took contracts that sent him around the world.

The travel fed the cooking. In Jamaica he watched locals make dishes like red snapper, and he brought those flavors home with him.

"I don't believe in luck. I believe in hard work!"
Qwan Butts, owner
The plates

Sold Out Every Time

Tired of time on the road, he started cooking for friends and family and selling plates on social media. They always sold out.

He tried trucking and flipping land, but his heart wasn't in either. Then a friend who knew his cooking offered him a small spot in his hometown that had belonged to her late grandmother. He prayed on it and went all in.

The food

An Urban Feel on Modern Cuisine

Seafood is the specialty. The Philly Qroll, his spin on the egg roll, is the dish people come back for, alongside boils, melts and pastas loaded with shrimp, salmon or lamb chops.

There was no business plan and no business background, just trial and error. He wanted to show his daughters and his community that you can build something positive with your own hands.
